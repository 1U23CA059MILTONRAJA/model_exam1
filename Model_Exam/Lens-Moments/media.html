Photography tips

01

Camera settings

Start in aperture priority: use a wider aperture for soft backgrounds, or a smaller aperture when you want more of a landscape in focus. Keep an eye on shutter speed to avoid blur.

02

Lighting techniques

Seek soft window light or open shade for flattering portraits. In bright sun, turn your subject away from harsh direct light and use a reflector to gently lift shadows.

03

Composition

Try placing the subject off-centre, use leading lines, and check the edges of your frame. A clean background often makes a stronger photograph.

04

Useful equipment

A camera, a versatile lens, spare batteries and a lens cloth are a good start. A tripod helps in low light; the best equipment is what lets you stay present.
